# Simple Pasta Aglio e Olio

A classic Italian pasta dish with garlic and oil.

## Ingredients

- 400g spaghetti
- 6 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
- 1/2 cup extra virgin olive oil
- 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
- 1/2 cup fresh parsley, chopped
- Salt to taste
- Parmesan cheese (optional)

## Instructions

1. Cook pasta in salted boiling water until al dente.
2. While pasta cooks, heat olive oil in a large pan over medium heat.
3. Add garlic and red pepper flakes, cook until garlic is golden (2-3 minutes).
4. Reserve 1 cup of pasta water, drain pasta.
5. Add pasta to the garlic oil, toss well. Add pasta water as needed.
6. Remove from heat, add parsley, season with salt.
7. Serve with grated Parmesan if desired.
